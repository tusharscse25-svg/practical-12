Bipin Rawat

About

Bipin Rawat was the first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India. He was a brave army officer and served the nation with pride.

Early Life

He was born on 16 March 1958 in Uttarakhand. He joined the Indian Army and had a long successful career.

Achievements

  • First Chief of Defence Staff of India
  • Former Chief of Army Staff
  • Awarded many military honors
